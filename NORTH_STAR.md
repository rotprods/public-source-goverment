# NORTH STAR

## Mission metric

**Qualified Civic Loop Completion (QCLC)**

A qualified civic loop is complete when a non-duplicate, jurisdiction-valid public need reaches a traceable terminal state with:

1. evidence and deliberation sufficient for the process type;
2. a versioned technical proposal or an explicit, attributable out-of-scope/invalid resolution;
3. the competent institution or authorized actor responding under a frozen policy snapshot;
4. commitments/milestones tracked when action is accepted;
5. a public provenance trail explaining what happened and why.

Primary metric:

`QCLC = quality-gated completed civic loops / qualified civic loops entering the measured cohort`

Measure by territory, topic, process type and cohort. Never optimize the aggregate by hiding difficult cohorts.

## Companion metrics

- Need → competent-route median time.
- Need → qualified proposal median time.
- Proposal → institutional response median time.
- Commitment → outcome closure rate.
- Evidence diversity and source quality.
- Cross-group deliberation participation.
- Duplicate-resolution precision/recall.
- Ranking explanation coverage: 100% target.
- Policy snapshot reproducibility: 100% target.
- Appeal reversal rate and moderation latency.
- Sybil/manipulation incident rate.
- Search/retrieval recall on golden civic datasets.
- Accessibility conformance and task success.
- SLO/error-budget compliance.

## Anti-metrics

Do not use these as product north stars:

- time spent;
- raw DAU without civic value;
- comment volume;
- outrage/controversy;
- ideological conversion;
- follower growth as authority;
- ad impressions;
- virality without territorial/public relevance.

## Constitutional invariant

If a metric can be improved by increasing polarization, hiding minority positions, privileging a political actor, or making users more addicted without improving civic outcomes, it is not a valid optimization target.
