# GOAL

## G0 — Legitimacy before scale
Build a civic platform that remains defensible when government and opposition swap roles. Same public rules, attributable authority, frozen process policies and appealable decisions.

## G1 — Human entry, technical routing
A citizen starts in ordinary language. The system resolves topic, territory, jurisdiction and competent bodies without requiring administrative expertise, while preserving the original wording and explaining uncertainty.

## G2 — Evidence over noise
Prevent duplicate fragmentation, structure arguments/claims/evidence, distinguish factual evidence from opinion and preserve source provenance.

## G3 — Solutions over identity competition
Enable multiple versioned technical proposals to compete on explicit criteria such as feasibility, cost, impact, evidence and public acceptance. Never let political brand strength become an implicit quality score.

## G4 — Civic process engine
Support multiple process types instead of one generic vote: support, prioritization, consultation, expert assessment, participatory budgeting, initiative/signature flows, internal organizational decisions and future regulated ballot integrations.

## G5 — Institutional accountability
Give authorized institutions a real workbench for competence checks, admissibility, triage, official responses, commitments, milestones and outcomes. Public users see a simplified but complete accountability trail.

## G6 — Explainable discovery and ranking
Use a modular candidate pipeline inspired by large-scale social recommendation systems, but optimize for civic utility. Candidate sourcing, policy filtering, scoring, diversity, ranking and explanations must be independently testable and versioned.

## G7 — Semantic + graph intelligence
Use hybrid lexical/vector/graph retrieval for duplicates, routing, evidence discovery, related proposals, expertise discovery and knowledge navigation. Sensitive political/identity data must not be embedded by default.

## G8 — Rust-first correctness
New domain kernels, ranking engines, projectors, workers and security-critical services are Rust-first. TypeScript is preferred for product interfaces and where ecosystem leverage clearly dominates. Python is restricted to ML research/training/evaluation or libraries with no mature Rust alternative.

## G9 — Security and public-sector readiness
Threat model from day one. Build toward OWASP ASVS, GDPR/DSA/AI Act requirements as applicable, ENS-ready controls, signed supply-chain provenance, auditable authorization and sovereign deployment profiles.

## G10 — Evidence-first engineering
Never confuse plans, generated docs, Figma, mocks or structural checks with executable software. Definition of Done requires actual build/typecheck/tests/security/replay/observability evidence.
